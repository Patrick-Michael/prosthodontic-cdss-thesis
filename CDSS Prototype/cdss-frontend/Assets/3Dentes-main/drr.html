The exposure — film in blue, beam in yellow, the tooth's long axis in green. Drag to orbit.
Known and accepted: bone carries no trabecular pattern, because the model has no trabecular geometry — it is one homogeneous solid. The zirconia crowns on 19 and 30 clip to flat white: the beam is modelled at a single effective energy, and 1.5 mm of zirconia transmits about 10-9 of it. Both are consequences of decisions on the record in docs/drr-plan.md, not defects.
