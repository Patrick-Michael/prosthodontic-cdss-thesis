# Prosthodontic Clinical Decision Support System

**Master's thesis:** *Design and Development of a Dual-Facing Clinical Decision Support System for Shared Decision-Making in Prosthodontics*

This repository brings together the research, development, and demonstration materials produced as part of my Master's thesis in Global Public Health at Deggendorf Institute of Technology.

The project applies Design Science Research Methodology (DSRM) to explore challenges in prosthodontic treatment planning and translate the identified problems and solution objectives into design principles guiding the development of a dual-facing Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS).

The developed prototype uses explicit and externalized clinical rules to evaluate patient-specific findings and produce prosthodontic treatment options and cautions. A shared interface presents clinical explanations and patient-oriented information to support deliberation and discussion of treatment alternatives, while leaving the final treatment choice to the practitioner and patient.

An interactive edition of the thesis is available as a live web application, allowing readers to explore the research, its traceability Sankey diagram, the full thesis reader, and integrated demonstration media directly through their browser.
